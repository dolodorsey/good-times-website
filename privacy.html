Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 30, 2026

Overview

Good Times™ ("we," "us," or "our") operates the Good Times mobile application. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information when you use our app.

Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Location Data: With your permission, we access your device's location to show relevant venues and events near you. This data is not stored on our servers.
  • Device Information: We may collect device type, operating system version, and unique device identifiers for app performance and analytics.
  • Usage Data: We collect anonymous usage statistics such as screens viewed, features used, and session duration to improve the app experience.
  • Push Notification Tokens: If you opt in to push notifications, we store your device token to send you event alerts and updates.

Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect your name, email, or phone number unless you voluntarily provide it.
  • We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.

Third-Party Services

Good Times uses the following third-party services:

  • Supabase: For venue and event data storage (no personal data is stored).
  • Eventbrite: For event ticketing links (you are redirected to Eventbrite's platform).

Data Retention

We retain anonymous usage data for up to 12 months. Push notification tokens are retained until you uninstall the app or revoke notification permissions.

Your Rights

You may:

  • Disable location services for the app at any time in your device settings.
  • Disable push notifications at any time in your device settings.
  • Request deletion of any data associated with your device by contacting us.

Children's Privacy

Good Times is not intended for use by anyone under the age of 17. We do not knowingly collect information from minors.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact us at: hello@thegoodtimesworldwide.com
