Frequently Asked Questions

How do I find events in my city?
Open the app and select your city from the city selector at the top. The "Now" tab shows trending events happening tonight and this week. Use the "Explore" tab to browse by category like nightlife, dining, sports, and more.
Is Good Times free?
Yes! Good Times is completely free to download and use. Some premium features may be introduced in the future, but core event discovery and venue browsing will always be free.
How do I save events?
Tap the star icon on any event to save it to your Vault. Access all your saved events from the Vault tab in the bottom navigation.
Which cities are available?
Good Times currently covers Atlanta, Houston, Los Angeles, Washington DC, Charlotte, Miami, Las Vegas, and more cities being added regularly.
How do I get push notifications?
When you first open the app, you'll be asked to allow notifications. You can also enable or disable them anytime in your iPhone's Settings > Good Times > Notifications.
I found incorrect event information. How do I report it?
Please email us at hello@thegoodtimesworldwide.com with the event name and the correction. We update our database daily.
Can I submit my event or venue?
Yes! If you're an event promoter or venue owner, email us at hello@thegoodtimesworldwide.com with your details and we'll review it for inclusion.
